What is Muamalat Administration?

According to the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) Programme Standard for Muamalat and Islamic Finance, Muamalat Administration is one of the sub-fields of Muamalat (Shariah-based programmes), alongside Islamic Economics, Islamic Finance, Islamic Banking, Risk Management and Takaful, as well as Islamic Financial Planning and Wealth Management.

It covers four basic functional areas of business management such as Management, Marketing, Finance, and Accounting from Islamic and Shariah perspectives.
